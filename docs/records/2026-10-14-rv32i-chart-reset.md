# RV32I result-chart reset

Started: 2026-10-14. Status: closed. Code state: `github-pages` at `448e4e3`, with pre-existing documentation changes in the worktree.

## Summary

The four public §4.7 charts and their obsolete generator are removed. A new, unimplemented figure plan follows the current §4.2 experiment contract and §4.7 results, with every proposed core-comparison chart showing all seven comparable FF targets. This follows the closed [results-presentation](2026-10-14-rv32i-results-presentation.md) and [structure-rewrite](2026-10-14-rv32i-results-structure-rewrite.md) records. Future chart production is a separate follow-up.

## 2026-10-14 — Review and chart diagnosis

- The §4.2 contract has three cores, but seven FF targets: single-cycle and baseline at their only not-taken behavior, plus the advanced core under not-taken, taken, backward-taken, one-bit and two-bit prediction. The comparable clock-adjusted measure is modeled ticks, at certified FF periods 166/80/100. The current §4.7 measured panels are 22 fixed × 7, 15 generated × 7, 23 controlled core-gap × 7, and five longer branch patterns × 7. The eight expected-trap fixed programs are correctness cases, not comparable throughput samples. The 497 measured panel rows include overlapping definitions; they are not 497 independent workloads.
- The current core chart renders only single-cycle and baseline bars because it divides their ticks by advanced/two-bit ticks and leaves that denominator implicit at 1.0. It hides the advanced series and the other four advanced predictor policies even though §4.7's purpose is to compare all three cores and advanced branch modes. The branch heatmap includes only advanced policies; the memory chart includes only advanced/two-bit; the Embench chart shows two projected comparators while leaving advanced/two-bit implicit. These presentations fragment the main comparison and make measured versus projected status easy to miss.
- Timed SRAM, DRAM, ROM and NOR-flash are supported only by the advanced core in the present experiment; the 18-pair memory panel is a scoped extension, not a seven-target three-core comparison. Predictor-capacity results have only three advanced/two-bit settings. Full Embench contains 19 × 7 clock-only projections from historical cycles and no current-source rerun. Figures for those groups should not imply a three-core timed-memory sweep or fresh Embench measurements.

## 2026-10-14 — Replacement chart plan

The new figures should each answer a question already posed by the report. Every core-comparison figure must show **all seven FF targets**: single-cycle, baseline, and advanced NT/T/BT/1b/2b. A reference target must be drawn and named, never used as an invisible denominator. These are proposed figures only; none was created in this reset.

1. **Measured FF group overview**, beside "Results in brief": five aligned dot-plot panels for nontrap fixed programs (14 cases), generated families (15), controlled core-gap settings (23), longer branch patterns (5), and checked-in ELF fixtures (2). Each panel has seven labeled targets and plots the unweighted geometric mean of `target modeled ticks / single-cycle modeled ticks` over matched cases. Draw single-cycle at 1.0 and a 1.0 guide; lower means fewer modeled ticks. Use the same horizontal scale across panels, print each panel's case count, and state that generated/core-gap definitions overlap, so panel summaries must not be combined into one score. The first four panels' ratios, in the seven-target order above, are respectively `1.000, 1.027, 0.836, 0.754, 0.770, 0.780, 0.791`; `1.000, 1.261, 0.825, 0.732, 0.724, 0.757, 0.743`; `1.000, 1.171, 0.796, 0.722, 0.727, 0.721, 0.723`; and `1.000, 1.510, 0.921, 0.763, 0.769, 0.740, 0.732`. Recompute rather than transcribe these when drawing.
2. **Controlled mechanism contrasts**, beside the dependency/load-scheduling discussion: two small panels, each with all seven target rows. Plot `dependency-d1 / independent-long` and `load-use / load-scheduled` modeled-tick ratios, with both case names and the direction of the ratio stated. The first contrast is `1.000` for single-cycle and all advanced modes, but about `3.419` for baseline; the second is `1.000` for single-cycle, about `1.158` for baseline, and about `1.27–1.29` for advanced modes. Use separate readable axes and print the values; a ratio above one is the extra modeled time for the stressed case. These are selected matched contrasts, not a causal decomposition of a compiled program.
3. **Long-branch policy comparison**, beside the 64-iteration table: a five-pattern × seven-target matrix, with all three cores and all advanced modes in columns. Color each cell by `that target's modeled ticks / fastest target's modeled ticks for the same pattern`, but annotate its **absolute modeled ticks**. Name the row-specific denominator and mark the lowest-tick cell or ties. This exposes the changing best policy without hiding the single-cycle or baseline results. Keep predictor-capacity results in their three-row table; they vary a different factor and cannot be folded into this matrix.
4. **Full Embench conditional projection**, only inside the projected-results section: a 19-workload × seven-target matrix of projected `target ticks / single-cycle ticks` per workload. Show single-cycle at 1.0, name every target, and label the whole figure "conditional projection from older cycles × current FF periods" in its title and caption. Keep the complete projected-tick table next to it. Never blend this figure with measured group summaries or describe it as a current-source ranking. If this qualification cannot remain prominent at reading size, omit the figure until a rerun.

The four timed-memory programs, seven program-20 core/memory pairs, eight expected-trap programs, and three predictor-capacity settings remain in tables: their scopes do not support another full seven-target comparison, and their small matrices are already readable. The figures should supplement, not replace, the complete tables. Avoid host-second and physical-frequency comparisons. Use one consistent target order, accessible labels and contrast, and captions that specify workload count, denominator, units and evidence status.

Before any replacement chart is published, generate it from completed result rows rather than typed table values. Verify the expected case/target grid, oracle/verdict and structural-check status, matching source/binary for current measurements, certified period, and `ticks = cycles × period`. Treat the historical full Embench cycle rows separately. Compare every plotted number with the adjacent table, inspect the rendered SVG at page width, and run the focused documentation check and strict MkDocs build. Keep evidence paths and hashes in an internal record rather than the public page.
